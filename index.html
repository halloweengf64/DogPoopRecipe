How To Make Dog Poop

In this recipe, you will learn how to conjure dog shite, just like mother used to make!

Ingredients

  • Dog
  • Dog food (or any food the dog can comfortably consume and pass)
  • Water
  • Patience!

Instructions

  1. Get dog.
  2. Feed dog.
    3. Helpful hint: Provide water!
